# 3D Library → sites without an API (offline): the Send to Varstotas bookmarklet's message
# makes a waiting card; the archive downloaded from that page (dropped on the window) is
# offered with the card's credit; one of its two models unticked (real click), Import makes
# a component in Components → Printables; the Downloaded list shows it, ☆ marks it, Place
# puts it in the model, an export warns about its non-commercial licence. A model file with
# no card gets its credit from its name, the licence chosen in the list (CC0), Import places
# it. The licence filter hides the NC model; the same archive again is a duplicate (Ignore).
action tray_only Components
action components
wait 10
action models favourites
wait 10
action dl_send url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.printables.com%2Fmodel%2F1-chairs&title=Two+Chairs+%7C+Printables&author=Bob&license=CC+BY-NC+4.0
wait 5
expect downloads=(waiting=["Two Chairs [CC BY-NC 4.0]"] offers=0
screenshot shots/dl_1.bmp
action dl_drop ../../qa/fixtures/downloads/chairs.zip
wait 5
expect first=("Two Chairs" "Printables" "CC-BY-NC" 2/2 dup=false place=false)
screenshot shots/dl_2.bmp
raw click 234 330
wait 3
expect first=("Two Chairs" "Printables" "CC-BY-NC" 1/2
raw click 254 400
wait 5
action online_wait
wait 10
expect last=Some("ok Two Chairs.vrst models=1")
expect waiting=[] offers=0
expect favs=0 history=1
screenshot shots/dl_3.bmp
raw click 265 90
wait 5
screenshot shots/dl_4.bmp
raw click 235 384
wait 5
expect models=(true history models=1 shown=1
raw click 205 340
wait 5
screenshot shots/dl_5.bmp
raw click 302 599
wait 3
expect favs=1 history=1
raw click 178 599
wait 5
raw click 945 579
wait 10
expect credits=["Two Chairs"]
action export_attributions dl_export.obj
wait 3
screenshot shots/dl_6.bmp
# A model file with no card: credit from its name, licence chosen (real clicks), placed.

wait 5
