# The compact interface: no menu bar, toolbar, status bar or side bar; the menu ring (taps and a
# held press let go on an item), the dock, the panels sheet and the Measurements number pad.

view iso
zoom_extents
wait 10
action ui_compact
wait 10
expect compact=(true None false false)
